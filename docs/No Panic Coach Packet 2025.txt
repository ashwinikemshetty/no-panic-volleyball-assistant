GAME ATTITUDE:
Players stand at the end of the bench.
Coaches divide responsibilities (e.g., stats, game
management, bench management).
STATISTICS:
Upload all statistics into your team packet.
ROTATION SHEETS:
A courtesy sheet provided in your team packet.
WORK TEAMS:
Teams are responsible for providing: scorekeeper, down ref,
line judges, Libero tracker, score flipper.
All players must stay in the court vicinity unless there is an
emergency.
Any player who participates but leaves early:
Will be fined a percentage of the tournament fee.
Will sit out a set at the next tournament.
A second offense results in expulsion from the team.
DO NOT SCHEDULE OR JOIN TEAM DINNERS
UNTIL THE END OF THE SEASON.
END OF TOURNAMENT
Coaches are responsible for ensuring teams clean up their
bench and camp areas.
Balls and carts must be returned to the gym immediately.

Violations subject to disciplinary action include:

Substance abuse or harassment violations

Minor Issues: Handled directly by coaches
Serious Issues: Investigated; actions are decided

OPENING THE GYM
PROCEDURE CHECKLIST
Arrive at the gym at least 30 minutes before the scheduled opening
time.
If needed, open the lockbox using the code:
🔗 Lockbox Access Link
→ Please remember to scramble the code after returning the
key.
Check the cleanliness of the gym and ensure all equipment is in
working order (nets, floors, fans, dehumidifier, balls, cages, etc.).
Turn on all necessary equipment:
Lights
Exhaust fan (Court 2 column)
6–7 fans (Spring/Summer)
Speakers (Court 1, 2, & 3 only)
Televisions/laptop (set to the specific program page > press F11
for full screen)
Ensure all courts are dust-mopped and that dust mops are vacuumed
and emptied properly.
→ Note: Coaches are responsible for their own courts.
Confirm that all safety measures are in place:
First-aid kits (band-aids, ice packs)
Fire extinguishers
Rags on each court pole
Check bathroom condition:
Clean floors and toilets
Toiletries filled
Soap available
Trash emptied
Open the front desk and greet members as they arrive.
For building managers only:
Ensure all new members are registered and have signed a waiver;
provide orientation as needed.
Confirm that all staff members are present and ready to work;
report any absences.
Review the daily schedule to ensure all classes or appointments
are correctly listed.
Monitor the gym throughout the day for cleanliness, safety, and

GYM SOP
CLOSING THE GYM
PROCEDURE CHECKLIST
Check trash on Court 3, lobby, and bathrooms.
Pick up trash on courts and in the lobby area.
Replace trash bags in all receptacles if full or if food is present.
Sweep paper debris on bathroom floors and in the lobby.
Refill paper towels, toilet paper, and soap dispensers in bathrooms
as needed.
Disinfect and wipe down toilet seats and sinks.
Count and lock up all volleyballs — report ball count in Discord chat.
Ensure all ball cages are locked.
Straighten up chairs in the lobby; fold and put away all folding
chairs.
Place any lost items in the Lost and Found or Jewelry Lost and
Found.
Turn off the exhaust fan located on the column between Courts 1
and 2.
Turn off all fans.
Empty the dehumidifiers and replace the water collectors. Then
turn them off. (Spring/Summer only)
Check that the back door and side doors are locked.
Turn off all lights, including in the front door area.
Lock the front door and return the key to the lock box.
Turn off the AC unit at the rear of the building. (Summer only)
Switch off both the Power Switch and Main Power.
