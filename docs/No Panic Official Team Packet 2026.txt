PLAYER EXPECTATIONS
YOU ARE CHOSEN FOR NO PANIC VOLLEYBALL TEAMS BECAUSE YOU
HAVE A COMBINATION OF MENTAL AND PHYSICAL ATTRIBUTES THAT ARE
CONSISTENTLY DISPLAYED BY SOME OF THE TOP VOLLEYBALL PLAYERS IN
THE REGION. WE EXPECT YOU TO DEMONSTRATE THE FOLLOWING DURING
THE COMING SEASON:
1.Intensity and motivation.
Players must be prepared to give 100% in practice every day. “Practice as you
would play” is a phrase they will hear often during the season. All our players
are strong athletes with the capacity to play at the highest level, but there is no
substitute for hard work. Players will show respect for their coaches and
teammates by coming prepared to do their absolute best every time.
2. Mental capacity and emotional resilience.
Players need to perform well under pressure and be able to shake off
mistakes. The ability to put each game behind you and focus on the next
challenge is a vital skill in both sports and life.
3. ‘Coachability’ and the capacity to quickly
learn new techniques.
Players must be able to actively listen to instruction, adjust how they do things,
and retain the skills taught to them. Work outside of practice will help players
improve their skills even faster. If all players continue to study the game,
practice little things at home, review videos, and take advantage of other
opportunities to improve (i.e., open gyms, doubles, 4 vs 4 tournaments, private
speed and jump training), we can build a very strong team.
4. Dedication to the team.
A team is only as good as its weakest player; therefore, it is in each player’s
best interest to help their teammates play to the very best of their ability. In
practice, this means that we constantly enforce a positive and supportive
environment for learning. Players who are afraid of making mistakes will not
improve their skills. In tournaments, this means players let the coaches give
instruction while players give encouragement. A team’s emotional state on the
court directly affects their performance. Players who are a negative presence
on the court will find their playing time limited.
NO PANIC VOLLEYBALL’S METHODS ARE DESIGNED TO DEVELOP WELL-
ROUNDED PLAYERS WITH THE MOTIVATION AND ABILITY TO COMPETE AT
THE HIGHEST LEVEL POSSIBLE. OUR TEAMS HAVE BEEN CAREFULLY
SELECTED WITH EXCELLENCE AS THEIR GOAL. WORKING TOGETHER, THEY
WILL GAIN SUCCESS THROUGH THEIR DEDICATION, HARD WORK, AND 4
UNDERSTANDING OF THE GAME.

TEAM FIRST
VOLLEYBALL IS BOTH A MENTAL AND PHYSICAL GAME.
STRONG TEAM CHEMISTRY IS KEY — A COHESIVE TEAM CAN ELEVATE
PERFORMANCE.
TEAM-BUILDING ACTIVITIES WILL BE HELD THROUGHOUT THE SEASON TO
STRENGTHEN BONDS.
ALWAYS DISPLAY GOOD SPORTSMANSHIP ON ALL PLATFORMS
(INSTAGRAM, SNAPCHAT, X/TWITTER, FACEBOOK, ETC.).
NO NEGATIVE POSTS ABOUT TEAMMATES, COACHES, OR OPPONENTS —
VIOLATIONS MAY RESULT IN CONSEQUENCES.
KEEP TEAM-RELATED GROUP CHATS INCLUSIVE — AVOID FORMING
EXCLUSIVE SUBGROUPS.
THINK BEFORE POSTING — ONCE SHARED, YOU LOSE CONTROL OF WHO
SEES IT.
ONLINE BEHAVIOR CAN AFFECT RELATIONSHIPS, TEAM TRUST, AND EVEN
FUTURE OPPORTUNITIES (COLLEGE, JOBS, ETC.).
EVERY PLAYER CONTRIBUTES — EVEN FROM THE BENCH.
STAY POSITIVE, ENCOURAGING, AND ENGAGED DURING MATCHES.
ATTITUDE MATTERS — COACHES CONSIDER BEHAVIOR WHEN DECIDING
LINEUPS AND PLAYING TIME.
PLAYERS GENERALLY STAY WITH THEIR PARENTS DURING TOURNAMENTS.
FAMILIES MAY COORDINATE SHARED ROOMS FOR COST-SAVING IF DESIRED.
COACHES WILL SET AND ENFORCE TEAM CURFEWS — PARENTS ARE ASKED
TO HELP UPHOLD THEM.
SOCIAL ACTIVITIES DURING TRAVEL SHOULD INCLUDE EVERYONE ON THE
TEAM.
FULL TRAVEL POLICY AVAILABLE ON THE FORMS & DOCUMENTS PAGE OF
THE NO PANIC WEBSITE.

GAME DAY
ARRIVAL: PLAYERS MUST ARRIVE 45 MINUTES BEFORE THE FIRST
SCHEDULED MATCH.
WARM-UP: TEAM WARM-UPS START 30 MINUTES BEFORE THE FIRST
MATCH.
COURTSIDE: BE READY AT COURTSIDE 15 MINUTES BEFORE THE FIRST
MATCH BEGINS.
STAYING ON TIME: TEAMS MUST KEEP TRACK OF THEIR COURT’S
PROGRESS TO ENSURE THEY ARE ALWAYS READY FOR THEIR NEXT
MATCH.
⛔ PER CHRVA/USAV/AAU RULES, LATE TEAMS MAY HAVE TO FORFEIT.
CAPTAINS ARE SELECTED DURING PRACTICE BEFORE EACH
TOURNAMENT (2–3 CAPTAINS PER EVENT).
EQUIPMENT: THE TEAM IS RESPONSIBLE FOR BRINGING THE BALL
CART, BALLS, AND BALL BAGS FROM PRACTICE TO THE TOURNAMENT,
AND RETURNING THEM AT THE NEXT PRACTICE.
FAMILY HELP: PARENTS AND FAMILY MEMBERS—PLEASE HELP
COLLECT BALLS THAT GO OFF THE COURT DURING WARM-UPS.
LOST BALLS:IF A BALL GOES MISSING DURING A PRACTICE OR
TOURNAMENT, THE PLAYER WHO LAST USED IT WILL
OWE $50 TO THE CLUB.
PLAYERS ON THE BENCH MUST STAND AT THE END OF THE BENCH AND
ASSIST WITH EQUIPMENT AS NEEDED.
NO PHONES, TABLETS, LAPTOPS, OR HOMEWORK AT THE SCORER’S
TABLE OR WHILE SERVING AS LINE JUDGES.
WORK TEAMS (SCORING, LINE JUDGING, ETC.) WILL ROTATE
THROUGHOUT THE SEASON.
ALL PLAYERS MUST BE PRESENT FOR ASSIGNED WORK TEAM DUTIES —
NO EXCEPTIONS.
PLAYERS ARE ENCOURAGED TO RECHARGE WITH HEALTHY SNACKS
AND REST.
THIS IS ALSO A GOOD TIME TO CATCH UP ON HOMEWORK IF NEEDED.
